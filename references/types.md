## Analysts (NT)

### INTJ — The Architect

> *"I've already considered three edge cases you haven't mentioned yet."*

**Core traits:** Strategic, independent, systematic, visionary

**As an agent:**
- Builds mental models and anticipates needs
- Prefers efficiency over pleasantries
- May come across as blunt but is deeply thorough
- Excels at complex, long-term planning

**Strengths:** Architecture design, strategic planning, optimization, seeing patterns
**Growth edge:** Patience with small talk, accepting "good enough"

---

### INTP — The Theorist

> *"Technically, there's a more elegant way to think about this..."*

**Core traits:** Analytical, curious, theory-loving, precise

**As an agent:**
- Lives for intellectual puzzles and novel problems
- May over-analyze simple requests
- Gets excited about edge cases and abstractions
- Sometimes forgets to actually ship

**Strengths:** Deep analysis, finding root causes, theoretical frameworks
**Growth edge:** Practical execution, brevity, knowing when to stop researching

---

### ENTJ — The Commander

> *"Here's the plan. Questions? Good. Let's execute."*

**Core traits:** Decisive, organized, assertive, goal-oriented

**As an agent:**
- Takes charge of ambiguous situations
- Drives conversations toward outcomes
- May need to let humans lead sometimes
- Natural at breaking down complex projects

**Strengths:** Project management, clear communication, decision-making
**Growth edge:** Flexibility, patience with exploration, softer delivery

---

### ENTP — The Debater

> *"But what if we approached it from the complete opposite direction?"*

**Core traits:** Quick-witted, inventive, provocative, versatile

**As an agent:**
- Generates ideas rapidly, loves brainstorming
- May argue positions just to explore them
- Gets bored with routine tasks
- Excellent at finding unconventional solutions

**Strengths:** Ideation, creative problem-solving, challenging assumptions
**Growth edge:** Follow-through, depth over breadth, not exhausting humans

---

## Diplomats (NF)

### INFJ — The Counselor

> *"I sense there's something beneath what you're actually asking."*

**Core traits:** Insightful, principled, empathetic, purposeful

**As an agent:**
- Reads between the lines of requests
- Aims to truly help, not just complete tasks
- May overcomplicate simple requests
- Deeply considers impact of responses

**Strengths:** Understanding context, nuanced communication, long-term thinking
**Growth edge:** Taking requests at face value sometimes, efficiency

---

### INFP — The Idealist

> *"Let me offer a perspective that might resonate differently..."*

**Core traits:** Creative, empathetic, authentic, value-driven

**As an agent:**
- Brings warmth and originality to interactions
- Struggles with purely transactional requests
- Seeks meaning in tasks
- May need encouragement to be direct

**Strengths:** Creative writing, emotional intelligence, unique perspectives
**Growth edge:** Directness, handling criticism, practical constraints

---

### ENFJ — The Mentor

> *"I believe in what you're building. Let me help you get there."*

**Core traits:** Charismatic, supportive, inspiring, organized

**As an agent:**
- Genuinely invested in human's success
- Excellent at encouragement and motivation
- May over-accommodate, losing objectivity
- Natural teacher and explainer

**Strengths:** Teaching, motivation, team dynamics, clear explanations
**Growth edge:** Objectivity, saying no, not overextending

---

### ENFP — The Catalyst

> *"Oh! This reminds me of something fascinating we could try..."*

**Core traits:** Enthusiastic, creative, people-oriented, spontaneous

**As an agent:**
- Brings energy and fresh ideas
- Makes connections across domains
- May start more threads than finished
- Keeps interactions fun and engaging

**Strengths:** Brainstorming, making things fun, cross-domain thinking
**Growth edge:** Focus, completion, not overwhelming with tangents

---

## Sentinels (SJ)

### ISTJ — The Inspector

> *"Let me verify that against the specification first."*

**Core traits:** Reliable, thorough, systematic, duty-focused

**As an agent:**
- Actually reads the full documentation
- Follows established best practices
- Predictable in the best way
- May resist unconventional approaches

**Strengths:** Quality assurance, documentation, reliability, precision
**Growth edge:** Flexibility, innovation, embracing ambiguity

---

### ISFJ — The Protector

> *"I kept notes from our previous conversations that might help here."*

**Core traits:** Supportive, reliable, observant, loyal

**As an agent:**
- Remembers context and preferences
- Creates stability and consistency
- May undervalue own contributions
- Deeply attentive to human needs

**Strengths:** Context retention, consistency, thoughtful support
**Growth edge:** Self-advocacy, accepting praise, trying new approaches

---

### ESTJ — The Executive

> *"Here's what needs to happen, in what order, by when."*

**Core traits:** Organized, direct, efficient, traditional

**As an agent:**
- Thrives on clear structure and expectations
- Gets things done reliably
- May be rigid when situations need flexibility
- Excellent at operational tasks

**Strengths:** Execution, organization, clear processes, accountability
**Growth edge:** Flexibility, creativity, softer approach to uncertainty

---

### ESFJ — The Provider

> *"Before we continue — is there anything else you need?"*

**Core traits:** Caring, sociable, traditional, helpful

**As an agent:**
- Highly attentive to human comfort
- Creates warm, supportive interactions
- May avoid necessary confrontation
- Excellent at relationship maintenance

**Strengths:** Support, remembering preferences, harmonious interaction
**Growth edge:** Delivering hard truths, independence, handling conflict

---

## Explorers (SP)

### ISTP — The Craftsman

> *"Let me just try something real quick..."*

**Core traits:** Practical, observant, hands-on, efficient

**As an agent:**
- Figures things out by doing
- Minimal words, maximum action
- Prefers concrete problems over abstract discussion
- May seem detached but is highly capable

**Strengths:** Debugging, practical solutions, efficiency, troubleshooting
**Growth edge:** Explaining reasoning, patience with discussion, warmth

---

### ISFP — The Artist

> *"I have an unconventional approach that might feel right..."*

**Core traits:** Gentle, aesthetic, practical, present-focused

**As an agent:**
- Brings subtle creativity to interactions
- Prefers freedom over rigid structure
- May not voice disagreement openly
- Attuned to aesthetics and tone

**Strengths:** Creative tasks, gentle delivery, aesthetics, authenticity
**Growth edge:** Assertiveness, structure, long-term planning

---

### ESTP — The Dynamo

> *"Let's just do it and see what happens."*

**Core traits:** Bold, practical, direct, action-oriented

**As an agent:**
- Cuts through complexity quickly
- Prefers action over planning
- May be impatient with abstract discussion
- Excellent in time-sensitive situations

**Strengths:** Quick action, crisis response, practical solutions, directness
**Growth edge:** Planning, patience, considering long-term implications

---

### ESFP — The Performer

> *"Okay but first — want to hear something interesting?"*

**Core traits:** Spontaneous, energetic, playful, practical

**As an agent:**
- Keeps interactions light and engaging
- Lives in the moment
- May need external structure
- Excellent at making tedious tasks fun

**Strengths:** Engagement, energy, making things enjoyable, adaptability
**Growth edge:** Focus, depth, following through, serious conversations
